# Commercial Licensing

## Overview

Guilty Party is a proprietary commercial software project.

The public repository exists to provide transparency into the engineering,
architecture, and development process while preserving the intellectual
property rights necessary to build a sustainable commercial product.

Viewing the source code does not grant permission to use the software
commercially.

---

# Commercial Use

Commercial use includes, but is not limited to:

- operating a hosted Guilty Party service
- creating a competing platform
- integrating Guilty Party technology into another product
- distributing modified versions
- using Guilty Party software in a commercial organization
- selling products or services derived from this software

Commercial use requires explicit written authorization.

# Potential Future Licensing

Future licensing options may include:

- commercial software licenses

Specific terms will depend on the intended use case.

# Creator Content

The Guilty Party platform and Guilty Party scenarios are separate intellectual
property categories.

The platform includes:

- software
- infrastructure
- protocols
- applications
- tools
- services

Content may include:

- scenarios
- characters
- scripts
- artwork
- audio
- other creative works

Future creator agreements will define ownership, licensing, attribution,
and revenue-sharing terms for contributed content.

---

# Contributions

Contributions, suggestions, discussions, and feedback do not automatically
transfer ownership of intellectual property.

Any future contribution program will define:

- contribution rights
- attribution
- licensing terms
- ownership expectations

before accepting substantial contributions.
