# Guilty Party Ecosystem

## Overview

The Guilty Party ecosystem consists of three primary participant groups:

1. Players
2. Hosts
3. Creators

The platform succeeds when all three groups benefit from participation.

---

# Players

Players participate in interactive experiences.

A player may:

- Join private events
- Purchase seats in public events
- Receive a character assignment
- Interact with other participants
- Investigate evidence
- Complete objectives
- Influence the story

Players may participate:

- With an account
- As a guest
- Through private invitations

Accounts are optional but provide benefits such as:

- Saved preferences
- Event history
- Payment methods
- Accessibility settings
- Friend relationships
- Reputation and achievements

---

# Hosts

Hosts are responsible for running live experiences.

A host may be:

- A friend running a private event
- A professional entertainer
- A community member
- A creator hosting their own work

Hosts provide:

- Story pacing
- Player guidance
- Performance
- Improvisation
- Atmosphere

The platform should help hosts by providing:

- Host controls
- Scenario preparation tools
- AI Stage Manager assistance
- Session analytics
- Player support tools

---

# Creators

Creators design and publish scenarios.

Creators may include:

- Writers
- Game designers
- Actors
- Theater professionals
- Independent creators
- Studios

Creators provide:

- Story concepts
- Characters
- Evidence
- Objectives
- Branching logic
- Artwork
- Audio
- Themes

The creator ecosystem allows Guilty Party to scale beyond internally produced content.

---

# Creator Marketplace

Future functionality may include:

- Scenario publishing
- Reviews
- Licensing
- Revenue sharing
- Creator profiles
- Scenario subscriptions
- Professional production partnerships

Creators should retain ownership of their intellectual property.

Guilty Party provides the platform and distribution.

---

# Relationships Between Roles

A single person may participate in multiple roles.

Examples:

## Player → Host

A player enjoys hosting a private event and becomes a community host.

## Host → Creator

A host develops original scenarios and publishes them.

## Creator → Host

A creator runs official performances of their own work.

The platform should support these transitions naturally.

---

# Economic Model

Potential revenue sources include:

- Private event platform fees
- Professional hosted events
- Scenario purchases
- Creator marketplace fees
- Premium subscriptions
- Enterprise/team experiences

The platform should align incentives:

Players receive value.

Hosts earn compensation.

Creators are rewarded for quality content.

Guilty Party succeeds when the ecosystem succeeds.
