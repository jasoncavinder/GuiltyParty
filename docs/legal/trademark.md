# Trademark Planning Notes

## Status

This document records planning considerations only. It is not a trademark
registration, clearance opinion, or substitute for professional legal advice.

No repository document currently claims that Guilty Party or related names are
registered trademarks.

## Candidate Marks

Existing project documentation identifies the following as possible future
marks:

- Guilty Party
- the Guilty Party logo
- product and service names
- major scenario names
- distinctive visual branding elements

## Current Usage

Use the product name as `Guilty Party` in prose. Do not add registration symbols
or make claims about registration status without documented legal approval.

Project branding must not imply endorsement, affiliation, or ownership transfer
that has not been authorized.

## Work Required Before Commercial Launch

Professional review should determine:

- availability and clearance in intended markets
- relevant goods and services classifications
- registration strategy and timing
- ownership and authorized-use rules
- treatment of creator, partner, and scenario branding
- domain and account-name conflicts

Search results or domain availability alone are not sufficient clearance.

## Relationship to Licensing

Software, content, and trademark rights are separate categories. Refer to
[licensing.md](licensing.md) and the root license files for the current software
and content licensing position.

This planning document does not grant permission to use Guilty Party branding.
