# Guilty Party Brand Guidelines

## Purpose

This document defines the initial brand identity principles for Guilty Party.

The purpose is to maintain consistency across:

- Product interfaces
- Marketing materials
- Creator content
- Host experiences
- Public communications
- Future partnerships

This document is a living reference and should evolve as the Guilty Party brand develops.

---

# Brand Overview

## Name

Official product name:

```
Guilty Party
```

Capitalization:

- Guilty
- Party

Do not stylize the name as:

- Guilty party
- guilty party
- GUILTY PARTY

unless used in a specific visual treatment where capitalization is intentional.

---

# Brand Meaning

The name "Guilty Party" intentionally carries multiple meanings.

## Mystery Meaning

The guilty party is the person responsible for an event or crime.

This connects naturally with:

- murder mysteries
- investigations
- evidence
- accusations
- reveals

---

## Social Meaning

A party is also a gathering of people.

This reflects:

- friends gathering together
- social entertainment
- shared experiences
- celebrations

---

## Entertainment Meaning

The name suggests playful intrigue.

Guilty Party should feel:

- mysterious
- social
- theatrical
- immersive
- fun

It should not feel:

- grim
- overly serious
- hostile
- exclusionary

---

Guilty Party should communicate:

## Primary Traits

# Tone of Voice

Communications should feel like a great host welcoming guests.

The voice should be:

- Confident
- Engaging
- Warm
- Slightly mysterious
- Entertaining

Examples:

Preferred:

> "Your invitation has arrived. The investigation begins Friday at 7 PM."

Preferred:

> "Everyone has a secret. One of them is deadly."

Avoid:

> "Users must complete authentication before entering the event."

Prefer:

> "Before joining the investigation, we need to confirm your identity."

Technical communication should remain clear, but customer-facing language should preserve the experience.

# Visual Direction

The visual identity should balance:

- mystery
- theatricality
- accessibility

Potential visual themes:

- detective notebooks
- case files
- invitations
- evidence folders
- theater programs
- classified documents
- dramatic lighting

Avoid overcommitting to:

- horror aesthetics
- gore
- true crime imagery
- excessive darkness

Guilty Party is entertainment, not crime simulation.

---

# Product Experience

The brand should extend into the product experience.

Examples:

Instead of:

"Session Started"

Consider:

"The investigation begins."

Instead of:

"Waiting for players"

Consider:

"Awaiting the remaining suspects."

Instead of:

"Game Complete"

Consider:

"The mystery has been solved."

The experience should remain understandable while adding appropriate atmosphere.

---

# Naming Conventions

## Products

Future product names should be evaluated for:

- clarity
- memorability
- relationship to Guilty Party
- trademark availability

Examples:

Potential:

- Guilty Party Studio
- Guilty Party Events
- Guilty Party Creator
- Guilty Party Stage
- Guilty Party Companion
- Guilty Party Academy

Avoid unrelated product names that weaken brand cohesion.

---

# Scenario Naming

Scenario titles should be:

- memorable
- descriptive
- genre appropriate

Examples:

Good:

- The Midnight Masquerade
- Murder at Blackwood Manor
- The Vanishing Ambassador
- The Asterion Incident

Avoid:

- generic numbered titles
- names difficult to pronounce
- names overly dependent on current trends

---

# Creator Branding

Creators should be encouraged to build their own identities.

A creator profile may include:

- Creator name
- Biography
- Portfolio
- Scenario catalog
- Ratings
- Host history

Guilty Party should promote creators rather than replace them.

---

# Trademark Considerations

The following may become protected marks:

- Guilty Party
- Guilty Party logo
- Product names
- Major service names
- Visual branding elements

Before commercial launch, trademark availability should be evaluated.

---

# Domain and Handle Strategy

Potential future properties may include:

- Official website domain
- Social media handles
- Developer accounts
- Marketplace identities

Brand consistency should be maintained across platforms.

---

# Brand Protection

The Guilty Party brand should not be used in ways that imply:

- official endorsement without permission
- affiliation with unrelated services
- ownership transfer
- misleading products

Community enthusiasm should be encouraged while maintaining brand clarity.

---

# Guiding Principle

The Guilty Party brand should make people think:

> "This looks like an experience I want to be part of."

The brand promise:

> Gather your friends. Play a role. Uncover the truth.
